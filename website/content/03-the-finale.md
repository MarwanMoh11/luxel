At the end of the night you stop choosing and the menu sells the game by itself, to charge one blast. Then you give the lantern the screen.

The chain is meant to read without the text. Each beat gets one plain line, and the picture does the rest.

## 1. THE SWELL

Once the last player menu closes, at about 4:40, the dark comes all at once. Rings of Snuffers arrive from every side every 3 seconds, each ring four bigger than the last. A band of night creeps in from the screen edges, and in it you can see the swarm's eyes.

`THE DARK IS COMING ALL AT ONCE.` Five seconds later: `YOU NEED MORE POWER.`

## 2. THE STOP

The world slows to a third of its speed, then freezes. A big coral line, then the answer under it:

`THEY ARE TOO MANY.`
`SELL EVERYTHING. ONE BLAST KILLS THEM ALL.`

## 3. CONFIRM SALES

The hidden ADVANCED tab opens with one row and no BACK: CONFIRM SALES, +2 HEARTS +25% DAMAGE, NEW: BLAST. Sell it and the padlocks on AUDIO and GRAPHICS crack one by one, each with a glass sound, and the header changes to EVERYTHING MUST GO.

`HOLD ON. THE BLAST IS CHARGING.`

## 4. THE MENU SELLS THE GAME

Thirteen or more times (one blast per setting still standing), a few seconds apart, the menu pops open on its own. A ghost cursor steps down to a row, a SOLD stamp slams over it, and the game gets a little worse. Each sale charges the blast (BLAST 3/13 or more blinks over the lantern) and the lantern glows brighter.

## 5. THE BLAST

The world freezes. Wick raises the lantern, and a cream ring rolls out past the farthest corner of the screen. Every enemy on the island dies as it passes.

`THE DARK IS GONE. BUT THE SCREEN IS BROKEN.`

## 6. THE RELIGHT

`THE LANTERN CAN FIX IT. MASH SPACE.` Under Wick: `SPACE: FIX THE SCREEN`.

Twelve presses. Each one restores more of the screen in a circle from the lantern and plays the next lantern note, rising through D harmonic minor. `THERE IT IS.` The island is whole again: colours, light, animation, 60 frames a second. Music and sound stay sold.

Then the price: `THE LANTERN USED UP ITS LIGHT.` It drops to its dimmest glow and a quarter of its damage.

## 7. THE REST OF THE DARK

Three calm seconds on the restored island. Then `HERE COMES THE REST OF THE DARK.`

The second tide is tougher and closer. The night band closes in from the edges until only a pocket around Wick is left, closing faster with each heart you lose. The lantern sputters. `LAST HEART` blinks over Wick, whatever the HUD shows.

## 8. THE SECOND STOP

`THE LANTERN CANNOT DO THIS ALONE.`
`GIVE IT YOUR SCREEN.`

## 9. PIXELS

One setting left on ADVANCED: PIXELS 100%. It opens as a small panel at the top, so the island, Wick and the lantern stay in view while you sell them.

Each Space sells 1% of the screen for +3% damage and +2% fire rate, and plays the next of 100 lantern notes. After five presses an AUTO-SELL row appears: the rest sell themselves while Wick fights, faster and faster. Every pixel sold pushes the night back and gives the lantern its strength back. The lines follow the pixels:

`1% AT A TIME.` / `EVERY PIXEL IS MORE POWER.` / `THE LANTERN IS FIGHTING BACK.` / `GIVE IT EVERYTHING.` / `LAST PIXEL.`

## 10. DISPLAY: OFF

The screen is black. The lantern stays lit alone, and around it the enemies' eyes stay open.

## 11. THE ENDING

About a minute, told in light. A press shows PRESS AGAIN TO SKIP.

- The eyes stop, blink, and creep into a ring around the last light. `THEY CAN STILL SEE ONE LIGHT.`
- More pour in and close over the lantern. It dims to three pixels, then out. Wick doesn't blow it out: the dark eats it. `THE DARK ATE THE LAST LIGHT.`
- The eyes close one by one. Eight seconds of black and silence.
- One gold pixel in the lantern glass. Every eye snaps open at once.
- The lantern rekindles, pixel by pixel, and bursts. A gold ring pops every eye on the screen. `THE LANTERN KEPT EVERYTHING YOU SOLD.` / `IT WAS TOO MUCH LIGHT FOR THE DARK.`
- `DISPLAY: ON`. The island comes back pixel by pixel, still in two colours.
- Then the credits buy the settings back, one per bar of the ending theme: MUSIC: ON, COLOURS: 16, RESOLUTION: FULL, FRAME RATE: 60, SOUND: ON, BRIGHTNESS: 100%, and on to UP KEY: ON (THE UP KEY IS BACK. LOOK AFTER IT.) and SUBTITLES: ON (STILL NO SUBTITLES. SORRY.).
- On the last bar the theme lands in D major, and LUXEL builds itself out of light that flies from Wick's lantern. `SEE YOU AT SUNSET.`

## WHY IT ENDS THIS WAY

The story's premise is that DISPLAY: OFF is the sacrifice and the win at once. The dark swallows the last light, but the lantern kept every setting you sold, a whole game's worth of light, more than the dark can eat.
