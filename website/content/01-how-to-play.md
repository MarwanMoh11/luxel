You only move. The lantern aims at the nearest enemy and fires by itself, so your one job in play is where Wick stands.

## KEYS

| Key | In play | In menus and cards |
|---|---|---|
| Arrows / WASD | Move Wick (8 directions) | Up/Down picks a row, Left/Right changes tab |
| Space / Enter | Only in the finale, when the game asks | Sells the picked row, starts the game, works the cards |
| Esc / P | Pause | Esc on the death card goes back to the title |
| Hold R for 0.5 s | Restart the run (a ring fills over Wick) | R restarts at once on the pause and death cards |
| F11 / Alt+Enter | Fullscreen | Fullscreen |
| Any key or a click | | Starts from the title and skips the intro |

The pause card reads SPACE: RESUME, R: RESTART, Q: TITLE. Esc does nothing there, so a double press can't quit your run. R is a hold in play because it sits next to W and E. There is no mouse aiming and no gamepad.

## THE LOOP

1. Move. Snuffers walk in from the dark and the lantern shoots them.
2. Each kill bursts into light shards. The lantern drinks them one by one, and each chime lands a step higher up the scale as it fills.
3. When the lantern is full, it rings, the world freezes and the Settings menu opens.
4. Sell one setting for a power, or pick BACK to keep everything and heal a heart. A gold NEW: tag on a row means that sale lights a new weapon for the lantern.
5. The run resumes. The dark gets busier, new tabs open, and the next menu asks for something you'll miss more.

## A RUN

| Run time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Wick lands at the sundial. Move. |
| about 0:20 | The lantern is full. First menu. |
| 0:40 | The first Hush Owl |
| 1:10 | The first Magpie, carrying a stolen star |
| 1:30 | The first Parasol Toad |
| 2:15 | The first Sporecap lands nearby |
| 2:50 | The CONTROLS tab opens: now you sell your own hands |
| 3:00 | The first Gloam pack |
| 3:30 | Formations every 22 seconds |
| about 4:40 | The finale begins |
| about 6:20 | DISPLAY: OFF, then a cinematic ending of about a minute |

A first play from the title to the results takes about 8 and a half minutes, counting the intro and the ending.

## TIPS FOR JUDGES

- In a browser, click the game once before you start. The click gives it focus and lets the sound play.
- The first play opens with a 24-second story intro (any key skips it) and a tutorial of about half a minute in which Wick can't be hurt. Both play once. Later runs start straight away.
- Sell at every menu. The powers are what keep you alive: a player who never sells falls before act 3.
- Every level-up heals one heart until you sell HEALING.
- From level 12 (act 3, when CONTROLS opens) a hit on your last heart still can't end the run. Instead THE LANTERN SPILLS: the gauge empties and its shards scatter around Wick. You have 4 seconds to run them back in, then they're gone. The results card counts SAVED BY THE LANTERN ×N. Get that far and you will see the ending.
- Catch the Magpie. Killing it drops its star into the lantern for a free level-up.
- Dying is cheap. Space restarts in place, with no intro or tutorial, and the first menu comes again at about 0:20.
- The ending runs about a minute. Press twice to skip to the results.
- If the game loses focus, it pauses.
