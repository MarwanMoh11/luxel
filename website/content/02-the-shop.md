The upgrade shop is the game's own Settings menu. Every level-up opens it with the world frozen, and you sell one step of one setting for a power. You get stronger, and the game gets harder to play.

## THE RULES

- One sale per menu. Or BACK: keep everything and heal one heart. Once HEALING is sold, BACK reads KEEP ALL and heals nothing.
- Tabs open as you level up: GENERAL and GRAPHICS at level 2, INTERFACE at 4, GAMEPLAY at 8, CONTROLS at 12. A gold badge on a tab counts the rows you can sell there right now.
- From level 12 a hit on your last heart can't end the run. THE LANTERN SPILLS: the gauge empties and its shards scatter around Wick. Run them back in within 4 seconds or they're gone. The results card counts SAVED BY THE LANTERN ×N.
- Act 1 sells your comfort, act 2 your safety, act 3 your hands.
- Powers stack. Damage, fire rate, speed and range multiply; hearts, shots and pierce add up.
- The beauty rule: anything that changes how the island looks, how the music sounds or how the game feels is stamped NOT FOR SALE, with a padlock and the word LATER. For the first four and a half minutes the island and the music stay beautiful. The AUDIO and GRAPHICS tabs sit there, locked, from the second menu on.

## BUY BACK

Once you have sold 11 settings, the menu offers one way back. Once per run, instead of selling, you can buy one sold setting back: it returns one step, and you keep the power it paid. It costs you that menu's sale, and the row closes for good.

- The menu's header turns gold: SELL ONE OR BUY ONE BACK ONCE.
- Rows you can return show BUY BACK in gold. With the cursor on one, the quip reads COSTS THIS LEVEL. YOU KEEP THE POWER.
- 14 settings can come back: TIPS, SCREEN SHAKE, TIMER, LANTERN GAUGE, HEARTS, WARNINGS, DIFFICULTY, MERCY TIME, KNOCKBACK, HEALING, MOVEMENT, INPUT DELAY, AIM ASSIST and UP KEY. SUBTITLES and RAY TRACING have nothing to return, and LANGUAGE wears off by itself.
- After the purchase: BOUGHT BACK. POWER KEPT. The light streams out of the lantern instead of in, and the restored setting shows over Wick in gold, for example MOVEMENT: SNAPPY.

## WHICH SALE LIGHTS WHICH WEAPON

Wick has one weapon, the lantern. Its shots take new forms as its stats grow, and each form switches on when a stat passes a threshold, so the first sale of each kind lights one. The menu shows it before you buy (+8% FIRE RATE  NEW: FIREFLY), and the tag shows over Wick for 2 seconds after.

| First sale | Lights |
|---|---|
| SUBTITLES (fire rate) | FIREFLY |
| TIPS (damage) | BELL TOLL |
| SCREEN SHAKE (range) | MOON TRAIL |
| RAY TRACING (speed) | RIVER WAKE |
| LANTERN GAUGE (more fire rate) | FIREFLY 2 |
| WARNINGS (pierce) | STAR NEEDLE |
| LANGUAGE (extra shot) | PRISM SHARDS |

Higher tiers follow as the powers stack. In a typical run, BELL TOLL 2 arrives with DIFFICULTY HARD, MOON TRAIL 2 with KNOCKBACK, FIREFLY 3 (five flies) with CRUEL, and BELL TOLL 3 with ICE. What each form does is on the ISLAND tab.
