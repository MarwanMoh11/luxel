## ONE LINE

A survivor game where the upgrade shop is the Settings menu. Sell the game itself for power.

## SHORT DESCRIPTION

You are Wick, a lamplighter moth with the last lantern. Every level-up opens the game's Settings menu: sell the subtitles, your hearts, your Up key for power. At the end of the night the menu sells the rest itself, down to the last pixel.

## LONG DESCRIPTION

Luxel is a short survivor game about the trade-off in every settings menu. You are Wick, a lamplighter moth carrying the last lantern across an island at the last minute of sunset. You only move. The lantern aims and fires by itself, and every kill sends light back into it.

When the lantern fills, the game's own Settings menu opens, and that menu is the shop. Sell the subtitles for fire rate, the HUD for damage, the difficulty, your healing, your grip on the ground, your own Up key. Each sale makes you stronger and the game harder to play, and the best-looking settings stay locked away until the end of the night.

Then the dark comes all at once, and the menu starts selling the game by itself: the music, the colours, the frame rate, the lights. The last setting is the screen, and you sell it one pixel at a time. A run takes about six and a half minutes. Made in Godot for a game jam with the theme Trade-off.

## FEATURES

- An upgrade shop that is the game's own Settings menu, with 19 sales to choose and a quip for every one
- Arrows or WASD to move; the lantern aims and fires by itself
- A pixel island in 20 colours, drawn entirely in code, that loses its looks one setting at a time
- An original soundtrack in D harmonic minor that follows your run phrase by phrase
- A finale where the menu sells the game by itself, down to DISPLAY: OFF, and a cinematic ending
