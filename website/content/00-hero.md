## HOOK

A survivor game where the upgrade shop is the game's own Settings menu. Sell a setting, get power.

## TAGLINE

THE LANTERN WILL TAKE ANYTHING THAT GLOWS.
YOUR SCREEN GLOWS.

## PITCH

You are Wick, a lamplighter moth carrying the last lantern across an island at the last minute of sunset. The lantern aims and fires by itself; you only move, and every kill feeds it light. Each time it fills, the game's Settings menu opens and you sell a piece of the game for power: the subtitles, the HUD, the difficulty, your own Up key, and at the end of the night, the screen itself, one pixel at a time.

## THEME

In this game, the trade-off is the settings menu. Every graphics and audio setting trades quality for performance. Luxel asks you to trade the game itself for yours.
