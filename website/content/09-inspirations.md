Luxel is a survivor game in the line of Vampire Survivors, with one rule flipped: the upgrade shop is the Settings menu.

## GAMES

| Influence | Where it shows up in Luxel |
|---|---|
| Vampire Survivors | The familiar game underneath: you only move, the weapon fires itself, kills feed level-ups, and each level-up is a choice. Its driving soundtrack set the bar for the run music. |
| Castlevania | The gothic colour of the score: harpsichord, organ and a tower bell, moved to a lantern-lit island at dusk. |
| A Link to the Past, Eastward | The view: top-down three-quarters, the ground seen from above and everything upright showing its face. |
| Peggle | The light shards: each one rings a little higher as the lantern fills. |
