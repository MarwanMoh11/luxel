The soundtrack is Lantern Gothic: a galloping minor hook on a square lead over harpsichord, a pumping bass, organ on the backbeat and a tower bell on the phrase tops, in D harmonic minor at 144 BPM. In the game it follows the run's clock phrase by phrase.

## THE ARC OF A RUN

| Part | Sound |
|---|---|
| Lantern | The hook at half speed on the lantern's chime, over a drone. The run starts quiet. |
| The theme | The full band: the hook, a lift to F major where the organ takes the tune and a choir comes in, a breakdown, the hook again |
| Night falls | A breath with a heartbeat kick, then the hook at half speed in D phrygian, with E flat, timpani and a tolling bell, then at full drive |
| Last light | A turn on A7 into D major: choir, glockenspiel, the lantern's sparkle, four on the floor |

The title has its own calm cue, a nocturne: the theme's second tune at half speed on a music box, over a rocking harp, a soft organ pad, a low bass and a tower bell. No drums. A 53.3-second loop, quieter than the run.

## MUSIC THAT WAITS FOR YOU

Menus freeze the world, but the music keeps playing behind the menu's wall, muffled. It never runs ahead of you: the score waits at the end of a phrase until the run catches up, so night falls when the run reaches night, however long you spend in the menus.

## THE SETTINGS ARE PART OF THE SCORE

- The menu wall: while a menu is open the run track sits behind it, low-passed at 500 Hz and 8 dB down.
- Selling MUSIC winds the band down like a tape in 0.45 seconds, under an organ sigh and one low bell.
- AUDIO QUALITY drops everything through a 700 Hz filter and a lo-fi crush: a phone call from down a well.
- SOUND sold: the coins of that sale are the last sound you hear.
- The lantern notes and the ending theme always play, whatever you have sold.

## THE SHARDS SING

Each light shard lands in the lantern on a 16th note of the run track. Its glass chime climbs D harmonic minor from D5 to D7 as the lantern fills, so the sound alone tells you how full it is. The shard that fills it lands on the top D and sets off a rolled A major chord, the dominant, and the level-up sting resolves it. The design doc names the models: Thresh's soul pull, and Peggle's rising pitch. When the arc changes key, the shard chimes follow it: E becomes E flat for Night, and F and B flat become F sharp and B for Last light.

## THE LANTERN NOTES

The lantern has its own voice: ten recorded notes from A4 to D6. They play the 100 notes of the PIXELS sale, one per pixel, over a drone. Mashing Space to fix the screen climbs D harmonic minor from A4, one note a press. When the island comes back in the ending, every tenth PIXELS note plays in reverse, like a rewind. On the title screen each luxel plays a quiet lantern note as it lights.

## SOUND EFFECTS

Soft foley from the Kenney packs (CC0), each sound pitched into the song's key and kept quiet under the music: no chiptune, no bleeps. The bell toll is a Kenney bell strike retuned to A, the firefly sting a Kenney pluck on D6, the padlocks crack like glass. The game ships 80 sound effect files, 35 of them for the shards.
